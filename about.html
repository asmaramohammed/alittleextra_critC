About A Little Extra

A Little Extra is a creative small business started by Anette. It focuses on bedazzling and customising everyday accessories using colourful gems and creative designs.

From keychains and hairbrushes to phone cases and bedazzling kits, each product adds something special and allows customers to express their creativity.
